Fuel the climb

Nutrition, made simple.

Train consistently, eat enough to support your goal, and keep your meals simple enough to follow every day.

Protein

Include a reliable protein source in your meals such as eggs, dairy, fish, chicken, lean meat, beans, or other suitable options.

Carbohydrates

Rice, oats, potatoes, fruit, and other carbohydrate sources can support training energy. Choose portions that fit your overall goal.

Hydration

Drink water regularly throughout the day and pay extra attention to fluids around demanding workouts or hot weather.

Consistency

You do not need a perfect diet. Build meals around minimally processed foods, vegetables or fruit, protein, and an appropriate energy intake.

General fitness guidance only. Individual nutrition needs vary; consult a qualified professional for personalized dietary advice.
