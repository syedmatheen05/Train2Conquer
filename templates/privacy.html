Privacy & security

Your data. Your control.

Train2Conquer should collect only what is needed to provide its features and protect account access.

Account information

Your account uses your name and email address for authentication and account functionality.

Fitness profile

Your fitness profile is used to generate and personalize your workout plan. Keep your information accurate and update it whenever your goals change.

Contact messages

Guest contact requests use email verification before the message is delivered. Contact details are kept temporarily in the session during verification.

Security

Use a strong secret key in production, HTTPS, protected form submissions, and secure environment variables for database and email credentials.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the profile menu. Account-related workout/profile records are removed by the application.
